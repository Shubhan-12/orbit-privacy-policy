# Privacy Policy – Orbit **Effective date:** November 25, 2026 ## 1. Introduction Orbit is a personal expense and budget tracking application developed by Orbit Labs. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how Orbit handles information when you use the application. ## 2. Information We Store Orbit stores the following information locally on your device: - Expense and transaction records. - Budget information. - Expense categories. - App preferences, including currency, theme and time format. This information is stored using the Room database (SQLite) on your device. ## 3. How We Use Your Information The information stored in Orbit is used exclusively to provide application features, including: - Tracking personal expenses. - Managing budgets. - Organising transactions and categories. - Displaying financial summaries. - Providing backup and restore functionality. ## 4. Data Collection and Sharing Orbit does not operate a developer-managed server for storing your financial records. Your locally stored expense and budget information is not uploaded to our servers. We do not sell, rent or share your financial records with third parties. When using Android or Google Play services, certain technical information may be processed by those platforms according to their respective privacy policies. ## 5. Data Storage and Security Your financial information is stored locally on your device. Orbit provides options to export and import backups in JSON and SQLite formats. Exported files are saved to a location selected by you and are your responsibility to protect. We recommend keeping exported backup files secure. ## 6. Data Retention and Deletion Your locally stored information remains on your device until you delete it, clear the application data, uninstall the application or otherwise remove the information. You can also manage your information through the application's backup and restore functionality. ## 7. Children's Privacy Orbit is a general-purpose financial tracking application and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. ## 8. Third-Party Services Orbit may rely on services provided by Android and Google Play for application distribution and platform functionality. These services may process technical information under their own privacy policies. ## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when application features or privacy practices change. Updated versions will be published at the same privacy policy URL. ## 10.
